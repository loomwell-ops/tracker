Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Template text: have a lawyer review it before you rely on it.

What we collect

Your email address, the job applications you enter, your resume text, and notes. Everything is typed in by you.

Where it is stored

Your data is saved in your account on Supabase, and a copy is kept in your browser on the device you use. Only you can read your account data.

AI features

When you tailor a resume, write a cover letter or read a job link, the text you send (resume, job description or link) goes through our server to Google Gemini to produce the result. We do not use it to advertise to you or sell it.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may access, correct and delete your data. Use "Delete my cloud data" in the app at any time, or contact us at [your email].

Security

Data travels over HTTPS and access is limited by your login. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid storing anything you would not want a resume service to hold.
